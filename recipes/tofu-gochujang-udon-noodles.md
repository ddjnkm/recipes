# Tofu Gochujang Udon Noodles

## Ingredients

### Chicken
- 6 oz chicken breast, skinless, boneless, raw
- 1/2 tbsp minced garlic
- 1 tbsp soy sauce
- Cooking spray (approx. 1/3 second spray)

### Tofu Sauce
- 3 oz silken tofu
- 1 tbsp gochujang paste
- Salt and pepper, to taste

### Toppings
- 1/2 Persian cucumber
- 1/4 yellow onion
- 1 green onion
- 1 portion udon noodles

## Steps

1. Thinly slice the cucumber, yellow onion, and green onion.
2. Chop the chicken breast into 1/2 inch cubes.
3. In a bowl, combine the chicken, minced garlic, and soy sauce. Set aside.
4. In a blender, combine the tofu, gochujang paste, salt, and pepper. Blend into a sauce, stopping before it thins out.
5. Coat a pan with cooking spray and cook the chicken over medium heat until cooked through.
6. Cook the noodles in a pot of boiling water, then transfer immediately to cold water.
7. Plate the noodles in a bowl, pour the sauce over top, and finish with the chicken and vegetables.
