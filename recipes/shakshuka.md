# Shakshuka

**Ingredients**

- 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 150 g yellow onion, diced
- 150 g red bell pepper, diced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 2 tsp sweet paprika
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1/2 tsp ground coriander
- 1/4 tsp red chili flakes
- 1 tbsp tomato paste
- 800 g canned crushed tomatoes
- 60 ml water
- 1 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp ground black pepper
- 6 large eggs
- 50 g feta cheese, crumbled
- 10 g fresh parsley, chopped

**Steps**

1. Heat the olive oil in a 30 cm skillet over medium heat.
2. Add the onion and bell pepper and cook for 6 to 8 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened.
3. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Stir in the paprika, cumin, coriander, and chili flakes and cook for 30 seconds.
5. Stir in the tomato paste and cook for 1 minute.
6. Add the crushed tomatoes, water, salt, and black pepper and stir to combine.
7. Bring to a simmer and cook uncovered for 10 to 15 minutes until the sauce thickens slightly.
8. Reduce the heat to medium-low.
9. Make 6 evenly spaced wells in the sauce with the back of a spoon.
10. Crack 1 egg into each well.
11. Cover the skillet and cook for 5 to 8 minutes until the egg whites are set and the yolks are still soft.
12. Remove the skillet from the heat.
13. Sprinkle the feta and parsley over the top.
