# Skillet Ground Beef Pot Pie

One pan, beef and root vegetables in brown gravy, butter crust on top.

**Serves 6** | Prep 25 min | Chill 30 min | Bake 35 min

## Ingredients

### Crust

- 1 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/4 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup unsalted butter, chilled and diced
- 6 tbsp ice water, plus more as needed

### Filling

- 1 lb ground beef, 85% lean
- 1 cup yellow onion, chopped
- 2 cups carrot, diced
- 2 1/2 cups yellow potatoes, cubed
- 5 garlic cloves, crushed
- 1 tbsp fresh rosemary, minced (or 1 tsp dried)
- 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
- 3 cups beef stock
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp Dijon mustard
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1 cup frozen peas, thawed

### Finish

- 1 large egg, beaten with 1 tbsp water

## Method

1. **Make the dough.** Whisk the flour with the salt. Cut in the butter until the mixture is crumbly with pea-size butter pieces. Add the ice water a little at a time until a ball forms.
2. **Chill the dough.** Flatten into a disk, wrap, refrigerate at least 30 minutes (up to 3 days).
3. **Brown the beef.** In a 12-inch cast iron skillet over medium-high, cook the beef uncovered, breaking it into crumbles, until browned, about 5 minutes.
4. **Soften the vegetables.** Stir in the onion, carrot, and potato. Cover and cook, stirring now and then, until they begin to soften, 5 to 8 minutes.
5. **Aromatics and roux.** Reduce to medium. Add the garlic and rosemary, stir 1 minute. Sprinkle in the flour and stir constantly 1 more minute.
6. **Build the gravy.** Pour in the stock, scraping up the browned bits. Stir in the tomato paste, Worcestershire, Dijon, salt, and pepper. Boil 3 minutes, then take off the heat and fold in the peas.
7. **Top and vent.** Heat oven to 400F. Roll the dough into a 12 to 13 inch round, lay it over the filling, tuck or crimp the edge. Brush with egg wash and cut a few slits in the center.
8. **Bake.** Bake until the crust is golden and the filling bubbles through, 34 to 38 minutes. Rest 10 to 15 minutes before serving.

## Notes

- **Beef fat is the roux.** No butter or oil is added because the rendered fat from 85% lean beef carries the flour. Leaner beef: add 2 tbsp oil before browning. Fattier beef: drain all but 2 tbsp of fat.
- **Potatoes.** Yukon Gold holds its shape and needs no peeling.
- **Faster topping.** Store-bought puff pastry, biscuit dough, or leftover mashed potatoes all work and cut close to an hour.
- **Make ahead.** Filling keeps 4 days refrigerated or 3 months frozen. Bring to room temperature before adding the crust and expect a longer bake. Tent with foil if the crust browns before the filling bubbles.
